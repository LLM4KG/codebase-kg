### Reconciliation with the provider statements

Checked against the Anthropic usage export and the OpenRouter activity export for **2026-09-18 and 2026-09-19** (the exports are not committed):

- **Claude, Phase 2:** the console's 18 Sep 13:00 UTC bucket (169,360 / 32,941) and 19 Sep 15:00 + 16:00 buckets (1,079,990 / 67,821) equal the rows above.
- **Claude, extraction (18 Sep, not in this table; see `wp9_cost.md`):** the 03:00–08:00 buckets equal the extraction logs hour by hour. The 02:00 bucket holds Todoist (829,594 / 16,327) plus 169,746 / 3,089 tokens ($0.56) from the two WP0 test runs, already recorded in WP1's cost check.
- **Qwen3-Coder:** all 70 (18 Sep) and 120 (19 Sep) generations, and per provider the call count, prompt and completion tokens, equal the rows above. Alibaba, Google and Novita are billed exactly as priced here. DeepInfra and Venice applied **automatic prompt-cache discounts**, and billed + `cost_cache` equals the figure here to the cent. The Qwen total was billed $0.1103 against $0.1206 here on 18 Sep, and $0.2531 against $0.4075 on 19 Sep.

So the prices and token counts are exact, and **the Qwen USD here is list price before provider cache discounts, an upper bound** (billed was 68% of it over the two days). The call logs do not record cached tokens, so the discount cannot be applied per call. The Claude calls had no caching. Days **other than 2026-09-18 and 2026-09-19** have not been checked against the statements: the July pilot before them, and the 2026-09-21 WP12 matched-ablation runs after them (`*_2026-09-21_wp12_matched`, $1.16 Claude + $0.13 Qwen). Those rows are priced by the same table as the rest and are included in the grand total above.
